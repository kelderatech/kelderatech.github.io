How We Work

Kept in the loop, every step

Most IT problems aren't really technical: they're communication problems. A server goes down and nobody hears about it until the printer stops working. A migration happens over a weekend and Monday morning is full of surprises nobody signed off on. Keldera runs every engagement the other way: you know what's happening to your own infrastructure before it happens, you're read in on every decision that affects your business, and you never have to chase down a status by calling and hoping someone picks up. That's not a slogan, it's a five-step process applied to everything from a single firewall rule to a full Microsoft 365 migration.

1

Discovery & Assessment

We walk the environment first , servers, network, accounts , and document what's actually there before touching anything.

2

Scoped Proposal

A written plan: what changes, what it costs, how long it takes, and what the rollback looks like if something goes wrong.

3

Scheduled Rollout

Work happens in windows you agree to in advance , never as a surprise after-hours change you find out about later.

4

Client Check-Ins

A short, plain-language update on what happened and what's next , no ticket-portal archaeology required on your end.

5

Documented Handoff

Every change is logged and explained, so your environment is never a black box only one person understands.
