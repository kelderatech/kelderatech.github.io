About Keldera

Built on real infrastructure, not a sales deck

Keldera Technologies is a Michigan-based managed IT and infrastructure provider built on 15+ years of hands-on, enterprise-grade experience, brought to organizations that don't have an enterprise-sized IT budget.

Systems administration, day to day
Focused on the infrastructure that matters

Our approach

Keldera Technologies was built on a simple premise: organizations that don't have an enterprise-sized IT budget still deserve enterprise-grade discipline. That means documented change management, proactive monitoring instead of reactive firefighting, and a security posture that holds up to scrutiny, not just a friendly help desk.

Our team's background spans hands-on infrastructure roles across automotive manufacturing, healthcare and emergency services, distribution and supply chain, and retail operations, environments where downtime has real consequences and structured decision-making isn't optional.

We bring that same discipline to every engagement: clear scoping, scheduled work windows, and a single accountable point of contact, rather than a rotating cast of subcontractors.
